## Featured ventures

### Goalance

> **The AI coach for training, nutrition, productivity, and consistency.**

Goalance replaces fragmented coaching workflows with one adaptive system that helps people execute their goals.

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/goalance-preview.png" alt="Goalance product preview" width="90%" />
</div>

**What it does**

- Creates personalized workout and nutrition plans
- Tracks training, habits, recovery, and progress
- Adapts recommendations based on user feedback and performance
- Connects fitness with productivity, routines, and long-term goals
- Turns complex health and productivity advice into clear daily actions

**Why it matters**

Most fitness and productivity tools give information. Goalance is designed to create **behavior, consistency, and measurable progress**.

### Simularis

> **AI-powered simulation and testing for companies.**

Simularis helps organizations test products, workflows, and decisions before deploying them into the real world. It uses AI agents that simulate realistic human behavior, enabling companies to validate experiences at scale.

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/simularis-preview.png" alt="Simularis product preview" width="90%" />
</div>

**What it does**

- Simulates realistic users, customers, employees, and edge cases
- Tests flows, products, support systems, and business workflows
- Generates structured insights, failure points, and improvement opportunities
- Reduces the cost and time of manual QA, research, and user testing
- Helps teams ship with more confidence and fewer blind spots

**Core technology**

Simularis is powered by **machine learning**, AI agents, behavioral modeling, and orchestration systems. The goal is to make simulated human testing scalable, repeatable, and genuinely useful for product and operations teams.

### Quantis

> **An AI hedge fund built for performance.**

Quantis is an AI-driven quantitative hedge fund focused on building high-conviction, data-driven trading systems. Its mission is to combine machine learning, market intelligence, and disciplined risk management to pursue exceptional performance.

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/quantis-preview.png" alt="Quantis product preview" width="90%" />
</div>

**What it does**

- Develops quantitative and ML-driven trading strategies
- Analyzes market data, signals, regimes, and risk exposure
- Uses AI to improve decision-making and strategy evaluation
- Applies systematic risk controls and performance monitoring
- Builds toward one of the highest-performing AI hedge funds in the world

**Vision**

Quantis is not about hype. It is about building a serious, disciplined AI investment system: rigorous research, measurable performance, and long-term compounding.
