## Goalance

> **Your AI coach for training, nutrition, productivity, and consistency.**

Goalance is being built to help people execute—not just plan.

### What it is solving

- Personalized workout programming and progressive overload
- Nutrition guidance, meal planning, and habit adherence
- Productivity systems that work around real training and life constraints
- AI coaching that adapts to progress, feedback, energy, recovery, and goals
- A unified dashboard instead of separate coaches, spreadsheets, trackers, and apps

### Product principles

- **Action over information** — clear next steps, not generic advice
- **Personalization by default** — plans adapt as the user changes
- **Beautiful systems** — a strong UX makes consistency easier
- **Real accountability** — feedback loops, progress tracking, and intelligent nudges
- **One ecosystem** — fitness, food, habits, goals, and productivity connected
